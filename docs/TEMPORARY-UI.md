# Temporary UI and ideas to revisit

Everything the user sees is the owner's to design (CLAUDE.md). Until the owner's designs arrive, Claude Code builds plain placeholder UI and makes the small UX calls itself (ARCHITECTURE §14.6). This file lists every one of those calls, so the owner can review and replace them, plus ideas the owner parked for later.

**Rule:** a step that adds or changes placeholder UI, or a UX behaviour the owner hasn't specified, adds a row here in the same step. Strings live in `src/AskiiArt.App/Resources/Strings.resx`, and colours and sizes in `src/AskiiArt.App/Theme/Tokens.axaml`.

## Ideas to revisit

The owner's ideas, parked until the stage they belong to.

- **Canvas-first layout, like Sketchbook and Excalidraw** (raised in M2 planning): the canvas stays the main character. Controls are docks that look like small floating widgets, anchored to part of the screen with an offset, like a Unity UI element anchored to the right with a position offset. The canvas paints the whole window, so art shows around and between the widgets and slides under them when panned. Widgets take up space all the same: centering, zoom-to-fit and scroll limits count them, and they always get clicks and other input before the canvas. On Windows and Linux the menu bar (File, Edit, View, Window and so on) is a small built-in strip along the top, whose fonts and colours can be customized later. On a Mac it stays in the system menu bar. The status bar is a small strip along the bottom, which a settings entry might turn off or edit. Those two are the only fixed things. The exact behavior gets designed with the owner in a later milestone.
- **Toolbar customizer** (raised after 2.2's testing as a "layout builder", and reshaped on 2026-11-04): a window for searching tools and dragging them anywhere, adding to toolbars or making new ones. Planned for M8.
- **Frosted glass and progressive blur for widgets** (from the docking brief): planned for M8, after a trial, with plain translucency until then.
- **Windows dragged out to a second monitor, Illustrator's collapse to icons, Unity's Panel and Collapse modes** (from the docking brief): parked (`docs/DOCKING.md` §10).
- **Minimap** (raised after 2.2's testing; **remind the owner when the work turns mostly to UI design**): an optional panel showing where the view is on a big canvas, as Aseprite has, for people using 4000 × 4000 canvases. It answers the one thing lost by having no scrollbars.
- **Decisions for the docking pass** (step 2.5): decided on 2026-11-04, in `docs/DOCKING.md`. Windows dock at the sides only, toolbars have a small grip, and toolbars and windows never share a tab or pass each other's height.
- **Eraser ideas** (2.4): a cursor that shows it's the eraser, and brush sizes (M3).
- **Layers panel ideas** (raised in 2.3): dragging rows to reorder, and icons instead of checkboxes, as part of the owner's design.
- **Debug menu in release builds** (raised in 1.5): hidden by default in release builds, but a setting, probably on an "Advanced" tab of the settings window (M8), turns it on for curious users and contributors hunting bugs. Today the Debug menu exists only in Debug builds.
- **Draw mode and text mode** (raised in 1.5): two primary ways of working. Draw mode works like an image editor, painting with brushes; text mode is a tool for typing text. Space + drag pans in draw mode only.
- **Document options** (raised in 1.5): the New dialog (1.6) chooses Unicode or ASCII only; document options should switch it later, with a warning that switching to ASCII removes every other character. Until then it's Document → ASCII Only.
- **Blinking caret** (raised in 1.5): blink at the system's caret rate where the OS has one (on Windows, the Control Panel keyboard setting), else every 0.5 s. The rate becomes a setting later (M8).
- **Rebinding the caret keys and jump sizes** (raised in 1.5; the jumps were built in 1.6): part of the shortcut settings (M8).
- **Typing pause as a setting** (raised in 1.5): the 1-second pause that ends an undo step, in text boxes and the Text tool, could be an advanced setting (M8).
- **How missing glyphs are flagged** (1.3): the orange and red cells are placeholders.
- **Removing the space option** (raised in M2b planning): the owner may drop the per-layer "spaces are empty" option one day. Nothing but the glyph plane of compositing depends on it, so removing it stays small (ADR 0036).
- **A custom colour picker** (raised in M2b planning): 2b.3a uses Avalonia's ColorPicker, which has a fixed layout that doesn't grow with its window. The owner decided on 2026-11-15 that we'll build our own, for precise control over its layout and each part. It's planned for M8; Avalonia's stays until then, for testing.
- **Reference image layer** (raised in M2b planning): an image layer to work from, which the eyedropper (2b.3b) picks colours from the same way as from the canvas.
- **Right-click paints the BG colour** (raised in M2b planning): maybe, for the brush. Right-click does nothing yet.
- **The Select tool** (raised in 2b.3a's planning; decided in 4.1): the selection tools don't replace the Hand tool. The shape selection tools are on M and the Magic Wand on W; the Move tool takes V in 4.5 and the Hand tool moves to H. Esc keeps switching to the Hand tool (ADR 0055).
- **How the selection looks** (4.1): the running dashed outline is a placeholder. Its look, its speed, and a tint over the selected cells are the owner's call.
- **Typing pause that follows typing speed** (raised after 4.1's testing): parked. A fixed 1-second pause is used for now (ADR 0058).
- **Color and plugins** (decided after 2.3's testing, ADR 0032): colour and ANSI are in, as milestone M2b. Plugins are in, as M9 (first M8b, ADR 0046), with a built-in Discord ANSI message plugin. Opacity isn't planned; wide (CJK) characters could be a plugin.
- **Tool Options toolbar** (raised in M3 planning): it's built from the active tool's options as data, so it isn't a plain container of tool buttons. It will probably be edited through a menu of its own rather than by dragging its contents, and tool settings should be exportable one day (like brush presets).
- **Blend modes for the Brush** (raised in M3 planning): Blend only for now. Replace and Darken only could come later, perhaps from a dropdown on Tool Options where they show greyed out until then.
- **Erasing text and backgrounds separately** (raised in M3 planning): the owner wants to erase a background without erasing the text first. 3.4 built separate erasers: soft and hard for text, and hard for backgrounds, since what a soft background eraser does waits for M5. A better way, such as a toggle on Tool Options, gets found in the UI Pass (M8).
- **How ramp strokes round** (raised after 3.4's testing; **decide in M8**): the Soft Eraser always lightens at least one level, so a low Strength still clears `.`, but 1% and 10% look alike. The Brush still rounds to the nearest level, so repeated soft strokes stop darkening their edge short of the intensity. The owner leans towards at least one level, and will decide for both tools in the UI Pass.
- **Pen pressure choices** (raised in M3 planning): 3.7 made pressure scale how strongly the Brush and the Soft Eraser cover cells (ADR 0054). Later the user chooses what it controls (size, strength) and perhaps a curve. Checked on a real tablet in M10.
- **The pen's eraser end** (raised in 3.7's planning): turning the pen over could erase, as it does in painting apps. Today it paints like the tip.
- **Shipped or measured ramp order** (raised after 3.1's testing): Standard keeps its classic `.:-=+*#%@`, though every bundled font measures `-` lighter than `:`. The user could switch a ramp between its shipped order and the order the font's measured darkness gives.
- **Ramps and brushes across the whole darkness range** (raised after 3.1's testing): letters and symbols ink at most about a quarter of a cell (`@` is about 25%), while blocks reach all of it. Other ramps or brushes could make use of that later.
- **Notice when a file changes outside the app** (raised in 1.7): a message when the open document's file is deleted or changed elsewhere, instead of locking the file (ADR 0024). It's on the roadmap for M10.
